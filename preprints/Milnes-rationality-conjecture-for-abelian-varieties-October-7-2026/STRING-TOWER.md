# The string tower: local connectedness of p-adic shtuka spaces via tilted convolution

**Idea:** proposed by [your name]. Build a non-minuscule µ as a tower of minuscule steps (a convolution, or "string"), push a good topological property down that tower, then up the level tower.
**Implementation and write-up:** with AI assistance (Claude). Six independent AI checks found no error: a review and two adversarial checks of the sketch, then two adversarial checks and a citation audit of this text. Their minor corrections are incorporated.
**Status:** not refereed. The gaps are listed in §7.

## The result

**Setup.** Let (G, b, µ) be a local shtuka datum over ℚ_p with b ∈ B(G, µ). Write C = C_p.

**Theorem.** Suppose µ = µ₁ + ⋯ + µ_k, where every µ_i is a *dominant* minuscule cocharacter for one fixed Borel. Then, for every compact open K ⊂ G(ℚ_p), the topological space |Sht_{G,b,µ,K} × Spd C| is locally connected.

**Corollary.** For such µ:
1. **(★) holds.** For every compact open K and every component C′ of Sht_K × Spd C, K acts transitively on the components of Sht_∞ × Spd C lying over C′.
2. **π0(Sht_∞ × Spd C) → Π := lim_K π0(Sht_K × Spd C) is a G(ℚ_p)-equivariant bijection of sets.** The two topologies may differ.
3. **Gleason–Lim–Xu Prop. 3.12 holds as literally stated:** G(ℚ_p) acts transitively on π0(Sht_∞ × Spd C).
4. GLX Thm 6.1(5), read as a statement about G°-torsors of sets, holds as literally stated whenever its Π-version does.

**Why it matters.** GLX's published proof of Prop. 3.12 uses Lemma 3.2, which is false. [GLX-STAR.md](GLX-STAR.md) repaired Prop. 3.12 for minuscule µ. [GLX-ALLMU.md](GLX-ALLMU.md) repaired every finite-level consequence for all µ, but not the infinite-level statement. This note recovers the infinite-level statement for every µ in the monoid generated by dominant minuscule cocharacters.

**Why the naive version fails, and what tilting changes.**
- The convolution of minuscule Schubert varieties is *not* a rigid space.
- Example: for GL₂ with µ₁ = µ₂ = (1,0), the convolution contains the open cell Gr°_{(2,0)} ≅ P¹(B_dR⁺/ξ²). The v-sheaf B_dR⁺/ξ² is an O(1)-torsor over 𝔸^{1,◇} with Hodge–Tate class ±dz ≠ 0, the Faltings extension. So it is not étale-locally trivial.
- Why that rules out rigidity: if B_dR⁺/ξ² were Y^◇ with Y rigid (take Y seminormal), full faithfulness of ◇ would make Y → 𝔸¹ a rigid-analytic 𝔾_a-torsor. Such torsors are locally trivial, since H¹(affinoid, O) = 0. Contradiction.
- The fix is to replace "rigid" with "covered by perfectoid spaces whose tilts are perfections of smooth spaces". Tilting preserves the underlying topological space, and local connectedness passes down quotient maps.

## 1. Two topological facts

- **(Q1) Local connectedness descends along quotient maps.** Let q: X → Y be a quotient map with X locally connected, let U ⊂ Y be open, and let C′ be a component of U. Then q⁻¹(C′) is a union of components of the open set q⁻¹(U), and those components are open. So C′ is open, and Y is locally connected.
- **(Q2) v-surjections induce quotient maps.** A surjection of small v-sheaves induces a quotient map on |·| [Sch17, Prop. 12.9].

So **a v-cover by a space with locally connected |·| proves local connectedness.**

## 2. The class 𝒫

**Definition.** 𝒫 is the class of perfectoid spaces Z over Spa C that have an open cover by Z_j with Z_j^♭ ≅ V_j^perf *over Spa C^♭*, where each V_j is a smooth rigid space over C^♭.

**(P1) Every Z ∈ 𝒫 is locally connected.**
- |Z| = |Z^♭| by tilting.
- |V^perf| = |V|. In characteristic p, V^◇ = V^perf by the definition of (·)^◇: a characteristic-p perfectoid space has a unique characteristic-p untilt (cf. SW20 Remark 10.1.2). Also |V^◇| = |V| [Sch17, Lemma 15.6]. Equivalently, Frobenius is a homeomorphism.
- For V smooth: rational subdomains of a smooth affinoid are smooth, hence regular, hence finite products of domains. Spa of a domain is connected, since a clopen splitting would give an idempotent by Tate acyclicity. Rational subsets form a basis.

**(P2) 𝒫 is closed under opens, disjoint unions, étale maps, and fibre products over Spa C.**
- **Étale maps.** An étale Z′ → Z tilts to an étale Z′^♭ → Z^♭. Over a chart V^perf = V^◇, [Sch17, Lemma 15.6] (V_ét ≅ V^◇_ét) gives Z′^♭ = V′^perf with V′ → V étale, so V′ is smooth.
- **Products.** (Z₁ ×_C Z₂)^♭ = Z₁^♭ ×_{C^♭} Z₂^♭, which is covered by V₁^perf × V₂^perf = (V₁ × V₂)^perf.

**(P3) Every smooth rigid X over C is v-covered by a member of 𝒫.**
- X is covered by opens that are étale over tori T^n (standard).
- Over such an open U, put Ũ = U ×_{T^n} T^n_∞, where T^n_∞ is the perfectoid torus. Its tilt is étale over (T^n_{C^♭})^perf, so Ũ ∈ 𝒫 by (P2).
- Ũ → U is a pro-étale surjection, hence a v-cover.

## 3. The convolution tower

Fix dominant minuscule µ₁, …, µ_k, and let Gr̃_{µ•} be the convolution over Spd C [SW20, Def. 20.1.3]. For the identification of the versions over Spd C and Spd E, see SW20 Props. 20.2.2 and 20.2.3.
- **(c1)** The convolution map m: Gr̃_{µ•} → Gr_{≤µ}, with µ = Σµ_i, is a surjection of v-sheaves [SW20, Prop. 20.1.4].
  - m is also proper: its source is proper over Spd C (same Prop.), and its target is separated [SW20, Lemma 19.1.4; Sch17, Prop. 18.3]. Properness is not used below.
- **(c2)** Each map forgetting the last step, Gr̃_{(µ₁,…,µ_j)} → Gr̃_{(µ₁,…,µ_{j−1})}, is étale-locally on the target a product over Spd C with Gr_{≤µ_j} [SW20, *proof* of Prop. 20.1.4].
  - This comes from étale-local triviality of G-torsors on Spec B_dR⁺(R^♯) over Spa R, for affinoid perfectoid R [SW20, proof of Prop. 19.1.2, first paragraph].
  - The mod-ξ step there is SW20 Thm 19.5.2(3) with Kedlaya–Liu.
  - Over a non-affinoid base, take the disjoint union of trivializing étale covers of affinoid pieces.
- **(c3)** Since µ_j is minuscule, Gr_{≤µ_j} = Gr_{µ_j} ≅ Fl_{µ_j}^◇, a smooth projective flag variety [SW20, Prop. 19.4.2; Prop. 20.2.3 over Spd E].

**Proposition A.** Gr̃_{µ•} has a v-cover by a member of 𝒫.

*Proof, by induction on j.*
- **Start.** Gr_{µ₁} ≅ Fl₁^◇. Take a cover S₁ → Fl₁^◇ with S₁ ∈ 𝒫, by (P3).
- **Induction step.** Suppose S_j ∈ 𝒫 v-covers stage j.
  - Cover S_j by affinoid perfectoids. By (c2), there is an étale cover S_j′ → S_j over which stage j+1 becomes S_j′ ×_{Spd C} Fl_{j+1}^◇. Then S_j′ ∈ 𝒫 by (P2).
  - Let F̃l_{j+1} → Fl_{j+1}^◇ be a (P3) cover. Put S_{j+1} := S_j′ ×_C F̃l_{j+1}, which is in 𝒫 by (P2).
  - Base changes and composites of v-covers are v-covers. ∎

**Corollary B.** |Gr̃_{µ•}| and |Gr_{≤µ} × Spd C| are locally connected.
- The first follows from Proposition A, (P1), (Q1) and (Q2).
- The second follows because m is a v-surjection by (c1), together with (Q1) and (Q2).

**Why dominance matters.** [SW20, Def. 20.1.3] takes the µ_i dominant. If a summand is not dominant, the convolution maps onto a strictly larger Schubert variety, and local connectedness does not pass to closed subspaces.
- Example: for GSp₄, µ = (2,1,1,0) = (1,1,0,0) + (1,0,1,0) is a sum of minuscule cocharacters, but not of dominant ones.

## 4. Up to every level

**Proposition C.** For every compact open K, |Sht_K × Spd C| is locally connected.

*Proof.*
- The period map π_GM: Sht_K × Spd C → Gr_{≤µ} × Spd C is étale [SW20, Prop. 23.3.3]. Its image is the admissible locus Gr^a_{≤µ}, which GLX write as Gr^b_µ.
  - Gr^a_{≤µ} is open in Gr_{≤µ}; see the proof of SW20 Prop. 23.3.3, via Thm 22.6.2.
  - For non-minuscule µ it also meets lower strata, not only the open cell.
- Being étale over a locally connected space is not enough by itself, since finite étale maps are not local homeomorphisms on |·|. Instead:
  - Restrict a 𝒫-cover W of Gr_{≤µ} to the open preimage of Gr^a_{≤µ}. It stays in 𝒫.
  - Pull it back: W ×_{Gr} Sht_K is perfectoid and étale over W, so it is in 𝒫 by (P2).
  - It v-covers Sht_K × Spd C. Apply (Q1) and (Q2). ∎

## 5. From local connectedness to (★)

Apply the theorem of [GLX-STAR.md](GLX-STAR.md) with F = Sht_∞ × Spd C, X = F/K and Y_i = F/K_i. That theorem is stated for normal rigid spaces, but rigidity enters only through (T0), (T1), (T2) and (T5). Each has a replacement valid here:

- **(T0) Separatedness.** Gr_{≤µ} is separated and proper [SW20, 19.1.4, 19.2.3], and π_GM has clopen diagonal. Over the affinoid base Spd C, quasi-compact opens are spatial, with spectral |·|.
- **(T1) Basis of connected opens.** Each Y_i is locally connected by Proposition C, and locally spectral. Take the component of x inside a quasi-compact open neighbourhood. It is open (by local connectedness) and closed in a quasi-compact space, so it is quasi-compact.
- **(T2) Open and closed maps.** Only quotients by the finite groups K_i/K_j occur. Sch17 Prop. 12.9 makes |Y_j| → |Y_i| a quotient map. Prop. 12.10, applied to Y_j ×_{Y_i} Y_j ≅ (K_i/K_j) × Y_j, shows the fibres are orbits. A topological quotient by a finite group is open and closed.
- **(T5) Classical points.**
  - *Density.* For Z ∈ 𝒫 with Z^♭ locally V^perf, the C-points of Z correspond to the C^♭-points of V, which are dense in |V| = |Z|. Their images under the continuous surjection |Z| → |Sht_K × Spd C| are dense. Components are open, so each contains a C-point c₀.
  - *Freeness.* X is separated over Spd C, so a section c₀ is a closed immersion. Hence F ×_X c₀ ≅ K × Spd C injects into F, and the fibre over c₀ is a free K-orbit. Likewise for Y_i.

Steps 1–5 of GLX-STAR then go through unchanged. This gives (★).

**From (★) to the Corollary.**
- **Surjectivity.** π0(F) → Π is surjective by [GLX-ALLMU.md](GLX-ALLMU.md) (L2).
- **Injectivity.** Suppose components E and E′ have the same image in Π.
  - For each K, (★) gives E′ = k_K·E with k_K ∈ K. As K shrinks, k_K → 1.
  - For e ∈ E, k_K·e → e by continuity of orbit maps (GLX-STAR (T6)). E′ is closed, so e ∈ E′, and E = E′.
- Equivalently, GLX-STAR Steps 2 and 4 show directly that the components of F_C are exactly the sets E_(D).
- Transitivity of G(ℚ_p) on Π, from GLX-ALLMU (T), then transfers along this equivariant bijection to give the literal Prop. 3.12. ∎

## 7. Gaps (none known to be false)

1. **Citation-level only: étale (not pro-étale) triviality of G-torsors over B_dR⁺.** It is needed because 𝒫 is not closed under profinite covers.
   - SW20 asserts it in the proof of Prop. 19.1.2, with the mod-ξ step given by Thm 19.5.2(3) and Kedlaya–Liu. SW20's justification is brief.
   - Backup routes: Heuer, arXiv:2207.07623, Thm 1.1, plus the Tannakian reduction; or a direct approximation argument giving étale-local sections of the smooth surjection P^an → Spa R.
2. **The toric-chart fact in (P3)** needs a precise citation.
3. **Extension beyond the dominant monoid of G.**
   - For example, SL_n has no nonzero minuscule cocharacters.
   - Passing through G^ad or a z-extension would need a homeomorphism |Gr_{G,≤µ}| ≅ |Gr_{G′,≤µ′}|.
   - Other µ would need a B_dR⁺ Iwahori / Bott–Samelson version of the tower, with (P¹)^◇ steps. No obstruction is known, but it has not been written.
