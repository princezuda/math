## Audit

An independent audit found no error in any step the paper argues itself. Two inputs to the §3 transport were checked against the sources. The decomposition taken from inside Kisin–Zhou's Prop. 2.1.9 holds, and the cited integral-model results are stated for p = 2; the paper should cite the locators. One citation, to Grothendieck–Messing, is wrong at p = 2, but the paper's alternative route is valid. Four levels down, Gleason–Lim–Xu's Lemma 3.2 is false, which leaves their Prop. 3.12 without a valid proof. [GLX-STAR.md](GLX-STAR.md) repairs Prop. 3.12 for minuscule μ, which is all this paper needs; that repair has not been refereed. See [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md) and the Lean [scope note](../../lean/docs/001.md).
